## 2. LinkedIn post

### Author

Chris Chen, Sales Manager at Reach Projector

### Copy

A projector RFQ should describe the room, not only the model.

“Please quote Model X” is enough to receive a price. It is rarely enough to compare complete solutions.

A procurement-ready projector RFQ should include:

- application and target image size;
- throw distance and mounting position;
- ambient-light conditions and expected daily use;
- signal, control and audio requirements;
- exact quantity and destination postcode;
- regional configuration, accessories and warranty needs;
- requested Incoterm and named destination.

These details expose differences that unit price alone hides: lens suitability, included accessories, regional firmware, chargeable freight weight, customs responsibility and the real delivery schedule.

Reach Projector supports retail purchases, wholesale sourcing and AV project requirements as separate buying paths.

# Materials needed from the owner

Provide these items once; production can then continue without repeated questions.

## Brand accounts

- Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and LinkedIn company-page URLs.
- Confirmation of the public account name and handle to use consistently.
- Account access must be connected through the platform or browser at publication time; do not send passwords in chat or store them in the repository.

## First-week B2C filming

- One clean wall or installed screen in a real room.
- Tape measure showing wall width and lens-to-screen distance.
- One standard-throw projector and one UST projector that are actually sold or used by Reach Projector.
- A safe power-off shot of HDMI, audio and power connections.
- A short room-light comparison: curtains open, curtains closed and normal evening lighting.
- A cabinet-depth and UST-placement measurement.

Record vertical 4K or 1080p video, 9:16, with the phone’s rear camera. Keep each clip 5–8 seconds, avoid digital zoom, remove customer information and do not show unverified certification marks.

## Product and compatibility proof

- Exact product model and regional-version label visible in a photo.
- Current firmware-version photo.
- Included plug, remote and accessories.
- Warranty territory and return terms from the supplier or manufacturer.
- For JMGO/XGIMI streaming claims: the exact TV-box SKU and the completed compatibility test sheet.

## B2B trust material

- Real warehouse, inspection, packing and shipment photos owned by Reach Projector.
- Real certificates only; never send a recreated or AI-generated certificate.
- One approved staff name and role for LinkedIn authorship.
- One anonymized project or wholesale case with country, application and quantity approved for publication.

## Publication handoff

When the accounts are connected and the above clips are available, confirm which post should go first. Live posting, replies and messages remain human-reviewed actions even when the copy and scheduling are prepared by Codex.

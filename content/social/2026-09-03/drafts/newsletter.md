# Your room is the first projector specification

**Subject:** Measure these four things before choosing a projector

**Preview text:** A model can look perfect on paper and still be wrong for the room.

Before comparing brightness, resolution or brand names, measure the space. Start with the available wall width, your preferred screen size, the real lens-to-screen distance and the amount of ambient light you normally keep while watching.

Those four details determine whether you need standard throw, short throw or ultra-short throw—and whether the screen should be white, ALR or a UST-compatible CLR material. They also reveal practical issues such as cabinet depth, ceiling-mount position and the path between your source devices and audio system.

The goal is not to find the projector with the longest specification list. It is to find the system that fits your room without depending on excessive digital correction or an incompatible screen.

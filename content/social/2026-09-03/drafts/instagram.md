# Carousel — 6 Checks Before You Choose a Projector

## Slide 1 — Cover

**Before You Buy a Projector:** 6 checks that matter more than the model name

Visual: projector silhouette, screen outline and measuring tape.

## Slide 2 — Target image size

Choose the screen size that fits both your wall and viewing distance. Bigger is not automatically more comfortable.

Visual: 100, 120 and 150-inch outlines on the same wall.

## Slide 3 — Real throw distance

Measure from the lens position to the screen. Confirm that the model can create your target image without relying heavily on digital correction.

Visual: side-view room diagram with lens-to-screen measurement.

## Slide 4 — Ambient light and screen

A bright living room, a dark theater and an outdoor movie night need different compromises. Screen type matters—especially for UST.

Visual: the same scene in three lighting conditions.

## Slide 5 — What you actually watch

Movies, sports and gaming prioritize different things: black level, brightness, motion, input response and connectivity.

Visual: three simple content cards; avoid unverified performance numbers.

## Slide 6 — System and connections

Check the regional system, streaming setup, HDMI ports, audio path and the devices you already own.

Visual: projector connected to a streaming device, game console and audio system.

## Slide 7 — The final check

Before ordering, write down:

- Country
- Screen size
- Throw distance
- Room lighting
- Main use
- Audio and source devices

**CTA:** Send these six details for a compatibility-first recommendation.

## Caption

Most projector regrets begin before the projector arrives: the room was not measured, the screen was treated as an afterthought, or the system and connections were never checked.

Save this checklist and use it before comparing specifications. A good recommendation starts with your room—not a bestseller list.

Research basis: recurring installation, screen, regional-system and connectivity questions in public projector-owner discussions. Product-specific claims require verification before publication.

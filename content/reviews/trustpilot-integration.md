# Trustpilot integration

Trustpilot is optional and must be connected through the official Reach Projector business account. The site renders the official TrustBox only when `NEXT_PUBLIC_TRUSTPILOT_BUSINESS_UNIT_ID` and `NEXT_PUBLIC_TRUSTPILOT_TEMPLATE_ID` are configured.

Trustpilot scores are never copied into product `AggregateRating` structured data. Review invitations must remain fair and must not be limited to satisfied customers or tied to incentives. API invitations and automated campaigns require a Trustpilot plan and explicit account authorization; until then the local review workflow remains independent.
